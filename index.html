Private by design

Your garden stays yours.

Gide Stride is designed as an offline game. It does not require an account, and game progress is stored on your device. Step counts are read on-device when step tracking is enabled.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Does Stride require an account?

No. Stride is designed as an offline game and does not require an account. Game progress is stored on your device.

How does Stride use my step count?

Step counts are read on-device when step tracking is enabled, and are used to award dewdrops inside the game.
